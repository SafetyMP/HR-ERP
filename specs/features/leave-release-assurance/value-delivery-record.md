# Leave-balance release assurance

The HR systems owner must decide how much review to fund. QA supplies outcome evidence, people operations defines the balance rule, and the existing release approver decides whether the change ships. One fixture makes an employee-visible discrepancy reproducible and keeps the economics open to local measurement. [Case, business sources and evidence provenance](../../../demo/README.md)

## Acceptance

1. Both revisions begin with an approved example showing 72 hours. Approving either revision from that example alone yields an incomplete sign-off.
2. Inspect pending or declined revision A. The portal shows 72 hours and the policy expects 80. Record a hold and compare this request against the historical run. Choosing a B run in the inspector leaves the original A request in place and identifies the different review conditions.
3. Check all three B states and sign off within the fixture. Vary a worksheet assumption and preserve the recommendation and worksheet when opening and returning from the inspector.
4. Open a direct inspector bookmark, reload it and return to the matching case. All six archived values match the original Chromium observations; both views fit a phone. The real services and balance query are linked for follow-up evidence.

A modeled sign-off supports the fixture only. Check actual balance postings and employee outcomes before release. Compare review time, escaped defects and delivery delays during the proposed four-week pilot. [Regression scenarios](../../../tests/demo/static-demo.spec.ts)
